## Key anchors verified
- Fear S1–S3 are a prequel. From S4, Fear runs concurrently with TWD (Wikipedia).
- **The brief's anchor "Fear S1–S3 ≈ TWD S1–S4" is not supported.** Fandom puts all of Fear S1–S3 in the outbreak year, alongside Rick's coma and TWD S1–S2.
- Fear S4E01: Morgan leaves Virginia after TWD S8. S4A also has flashbacks that cover the time between Fear S3 and S4.
- TWD S9 has two jumps: about 1.5 years at the premiere, then "six years later" at the end of S9E05.
- World Beyond is set ten years after the apocalypse began.
- Fear S8 jumps seven years after S7, inside S8E01.
- TOWL covers years of backstory since TWD S9E05 ("five years since being taken"; "six years after Rick's supposed death"). Its present day comes after TWD's end.

## Where sources disagree / are weak
- **Dead City gap:** SYFY reports "two years after the finale", based on Jeffrey Dean Morgan's comments. Fandom dates S1 to 2027, about five years after its own TWD-finale year, but its day count works out to about 2029. Recommended wording: "some years after".
- **Fear S8 vs TWD S11:** only Fandom slots Fear S8 between S11E12 and E13, so this is approximate.
- **Passage:** Fandom says "during Fear S2" and Wikipedia says "before Fear S3". The two fit together: end of S2.
- **Dead in the Water:** Fandom says it is set during Fear S1 and is a prequel to S6. Wikipedia says "before S6".
- **Althea Tapes:** mostly set before Fear S4, but the last part sits between Fear S5E08 and S5E09. Watch the whole set at that point.
- **Tales "La Doña":** Fandom says outright that its placement is a guess.
- **Order of TOWL, Daryl Dixon and Dead City:** these run in parallel after TWD. Only Fandom gives an order: TOWL, then Daryl Dixon, then Davon, then Dead City.
- **Fear S1–S3 interleaved with TWD S1:** no source gives a day-by-day interleave. Fear starts at the very beginning of the outbreak, during Rick's coma.
